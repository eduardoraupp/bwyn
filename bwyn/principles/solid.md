# SOLID

- SRP: one reason to change per class and module.                                                                                                                                                                                 
 - OCP: open for extension, closed for modification.                                                                                                                                                                               
 - LSP: subtypes are substitutable for their base types.                                                                                                                                                                           
 - ISP: no client is forced to depend on methods it doesn't use.                                                                                                                                                                   
 - DIP: both high-level and low-level modules depend on abstractions.
